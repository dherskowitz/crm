# Product

## Register

product

## Users

A small, flat sales team working inbound leads from a shared queue. They live in
this tool all day: triaging new leads, claiming ownership, logging calls and
notes, moving deals through the pipeline, and watching their numbers. They are
fluent in modern software and impatient with friction — every extra click or
slow screen is felt dozens of times a day. Managers and admins use the same
surfaces plus team reporting, assignment, merge, and configuration.

## Product Purpose

A custom CRM that captures inbound leads automatically, gives the team a clean
queue-and-claim workflow, tracks each lead through a fixed pipeline to a
conversion event, and surfaces the metrics that explain and predict sales
outcomes. Success is a team that trusts the data, never loses a follow-up, and
can see at a glance where the pipeline stands.

## Brand Personality

Precise, confident, quietly branded. The voice of an expert tool that respects
the user's time: dense where density helps, calm where focus matters, never
decorative for its own sake. Three words: **sharp, trustworthy, fast.**

## Anti-references

- **Default shadcn / generic SaaS template.** The thing we are explicitly moving
  away from: flat neutral surfaces, stock radii and shadows, no point of view.
- Heavy, "enterprise" dashboards drowning in chrome and gradients.
- Playful/consumer aesthetics — oversized rounded cards, mascots, confetti.
- Decoration that doesn't carry information (gradient text, glass panels,
  hero-metric templates, icon-card grids, per-section uppercase eyebrows).

## Design Principles

1. **The tool disappears into the task.** Earned familiarity over novelty; standard
   affordances done impeccably, not reinvented for flavor.
2. **Density with air.** Information-dense where the user is scanning (lists,
   tables, timelines); generous and calm where they are deciding (detail, forms).
3. **Color carries meaning, not mood.** Blue means action/selection/state; navy is
   the brand anchor; everything else is a disciplined neutral. No decorative color.
4. **Speed is a feature.** Keyboard-first (command palette, list navigation),
   instant feedback, skeletons over spinners, motion that conveys state in 150–250ms.
5. **Every state is designed.** Hover, focus, active, disabled, loading, empty,
   error — all first-class, consistent across every screen.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Target WCAG 2.1 AA: body text ≥4.5:1, large/secondary text ≥3:1, including
  placeholder and muted text (no light-gray-for-elegance).
- Visible, consistent keyboard focus ring on every interactive element.
- Full `prefers-reduced-motion` path for all motion.
- Light and dark themes, each independently contrast-checked.
- State is never conveyed by color alone (icon/label/shape accompany it).
